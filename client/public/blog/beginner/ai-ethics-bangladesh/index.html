1. AI Bias: When AI Gets It Wrong

AI learns from data. If that data contains human biases, the AI will learn and amplify those biases. For example, if an AI is trained mostly on English data, it will perform poorly on Bengali. If it's trained on data from one demographic, it may discriminate against others.

2. Privacy: Your Data and AI

AI tools collect data to improve their services. When you use ChatGPT, your conversations may be used for training. It's important to:

  • Never share sensitive personal information with AI
  • Be aware of what data each AI tool collects
  • Use privacy settings to control data sharing
  • Choose trusted providers who respect privacy

3. Job Displacement vs Job Transformation

The biggest fear about AI is job loss. The reality is more nuanced. AI will eliminate some jobs but create many more. The key is adaptation. Jobs that involve repetitive tasks will be automated. Jobs that require creativity, emotional intelligence, and complex decision-making will be enhanced by AI.

4. AI and the Bangladesh National AI Policy

The Bangladesh government's National AI Policy 2026-2030, developed with UNESCO and UNDP, emphasizes ethical AI. It includes frameworks for algorithmic impact assessments, data protection, and inclusive AI development that benefits all citizens, especially women, youth, and marginalized communities.
