The Bangladesh government has recognized AI as a strategic priority and is taking concrete steps to build a national AI ecosystem. Here's a complete overview of government AI initiatives.

National AI Policy 2026-2030

The draft National AI Policy 2026-2030 has been released for public consultation. Key highlights include: 10 priority sectors for AI adoption, establishment of a National Data Governance Authority (NDGA), development of a national Bengali language model, tax incentives for AI hardware imports, and ethical AI frameworks aligned with UNESCO recommendations. [[srcs: National AI Policy Draft: National Artificial Intelligence Policy 2026-2030 (Draft V2.0) ]]

AI Hub Project (Tk192.66 Crore)

With KOICA funding, the government is establishing a national AI hub to train 865 AI specialists, create 30 AI startups, publish 10 international research papers, and achieve 80% employment for trainees. [[srcs: The Business Standard: Tk192.66 crore project to establish a national AI hub ]]

National Bengali Language Model

The Bangladesh Computer Council (BCC) is developing a locally deployed open-source national LLM for Bengali, coordinating with universities, research institutions, and qualified private-sector labs.

AI Readiness Assessment

Conducted with UNESCO, UNDP, and EU support, the AI Readiness Assessment Methodology (RAM) report provides a comprehensive picture of Bangladesh's preparedness for ethical AI adoption.
