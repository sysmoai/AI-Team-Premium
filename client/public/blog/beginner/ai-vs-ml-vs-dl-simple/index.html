Machine Learning (ML) â€” AI That Learns

Machine Learning is a subset of AI. Instead of being explicitly programmed for every task, ML systems learn from data. You show them examples, and they figure out the patterns themselves.

Think of it like teaching a child: you don't give them a rulebook for every situation. You show them examples, and they learn. ML works the same way.

Examples in Bangladesh: YouTube recommending videos based on your watch history, email spam filters, Netflix suggesting shows

Deep Learning (DL) â€” The Brain of Modern AI

Deep Learning is a subset of ML that uses neural networks â€” computing systems inspired by the human brain. These networks have multiple "layers" (that's why it's called "deep") that process information at increasing levels of abstraction.
