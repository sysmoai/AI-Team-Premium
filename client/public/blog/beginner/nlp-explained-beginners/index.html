What is NLP?

NLP combines computer science, linguistics, and machine learning to bridge the gap between human communication and computer understanding. It's what makes Google Translate work, what powers ChatGPT's conversations, and what enables voice assistants like Siri and Google Assistant.

Everyday NLP Examples

  • Google Translate: Converts Bengali to English and vice versa
  • ChatGPT: Understands your questions and generates human-like responses
  • Gmail Smart Compose: Suggests complete sentences as you type
  • Grammarly: Checks grammar, style, and tone of your writing
  • Facebook: Translates posts automatically
  • Google Search: Understands what you're looking for even with typos

NLP for Bengali Language

Bengali is the 7th most spoken language in the world, but it's been underserved by NLP technology. That's changing rapidly. The Bangladesh government is developing a National Bengali Language Model, and companies like Google, Meta, and OpenAI have improved their Bengali NLP capabilities significantly.
