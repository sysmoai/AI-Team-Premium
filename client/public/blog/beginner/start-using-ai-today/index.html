4

Experiment with Different Tasks

AI can do much more than answer questions. Try these:

  • Writing: Blog posts, emails, social media captions, poems
  • Coding: Debug code, explain programming concepts, generate scripts
  • Analysis: Summarize articles, analyze data, extract insights

5

Create Your Daily AI Habit

Use AI for at least 15 minutes every day. The more you use it, the better you'll understand its capabilities and limitations. Start with one task per day:

  • Day 1: Ask AI to plan your day
  • Day 2: Ask AI to explain a concept you're studying
  • Day 3: Use AI to write an email
  • Day 4: Ask AI for recipe ideas
  • Day 5: Use AI to generate a social media post
