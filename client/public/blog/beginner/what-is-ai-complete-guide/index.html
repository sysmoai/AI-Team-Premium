3. How AI Works (AI à¦•à§€à¦­à¦¾à¦¬à§‡ à¦•à¦¾à¦œ à¦•à¦°à§‡)

AI works through a process called machine learning. Here's a simple breakdown:

Step 1: Data Collection

AI learns from examples â€” lots of them. Think of it like teaching a child: the more examples you show them, the better they understand. AI is trained on millions of text documents, images, conversations, and data points.

Step 2: Training

The AI studies patterns in the data. For example, by analyzing millions of Bengali sentences, an AI learns grammar, vocabulary, and context. It doesn't "understand" the way humans do â€” it recognizes statistical patterns.

Step 3: Inference

When you ask AI a question, it uses everything it learned during training to generate the most likely correct answer. This happens in milliseconds.

Step 4: Improvement

AI gets better with feedback. When you rate an answer, correct a response, or provide more context, the AI learns and improves for next time.
