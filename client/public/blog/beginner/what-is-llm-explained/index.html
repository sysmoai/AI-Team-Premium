How LLMs Work

LLMs use a technology called Transformers (introduced by Google in 2017). When you give an LLM a prompt, it:

  1. Breaks your text into small pieces called "tokens"
  2. Analyzes the relationships between all tokens
  3. Predicts the most likely next token
  4. Repeats this process to generate the complete response

Each step considers the entire context â€” so the model can maintain coherence across long conversations. This is why ChatGPT can remember what you said 50 messages ago.

Bangladesh's Own LLM

The Bangladesh government, through the Bangladesh Computer Council (BCC), is developing a National Bengali Language Model as part of the AI Policy 2026-2030. This will be a locally developed LLM that understands Bengali dialects, regional variations, and the cultural context of Bangladesh.
