What is Machine Learning?

Instead of writing rules for every situation, ML systems learn from examples. You show them data, they find patterns, and they use those patterns to make predictions or decisions.

3 Types of Machine Learning

1. Supervised Learning

You give the AI labeled examples and it learns to predict labels for new data. Example: Training an AI to recognize Bengali handwritten digits by showing it thousands of labeled examples.

2. Unsupervised Learning

You give the AI unlabeled data and it finds patterns on its own. Example: Grouping customers by shopping behavior on Daraz â€” the AI discovers customer segments automatically.

3. Reinforcement Learning

The AI learns through trial and error, getting rewards for correct actions. Example: An AI learning to play chess or Ludo by playing millions of games against itself.
