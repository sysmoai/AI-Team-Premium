Audience research first

  • "Build a customer persona for [product] sold in [area of Bangladesh]: age, income band, what they worry about, and the words they use."
  • "What Facebook interests and behaviours would this person have?"
  • "List 10 objections that stop them buying, ranked by frequency."
  • "What triggers make this person buy now rather than later?"

Ad copy that stops the scroll

  • "Write a Facebook ad for [product] targeting [persona]. Structure: pattern-interrupt hook, one benefit, proof, price in BDT, clear CTA. Under 90 words, natural Banglish."
  • "Write the first line 15 different ways."
  • "Give me the same ad in five angles: problem/solution, curiosity, social proof, urgency, identity."
  • "Rewrite this to feel like a recommendation from a friend, not an advertisement."
