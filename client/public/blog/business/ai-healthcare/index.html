The boundary, stated first

  • AI does not diagnose patients. Clinical decisions belong to qualified clinicians.
  • AI does not prescribe. Ever.
  • Patient data is confidential. Never upload identifiable patient information to a third-party service.
  • Training cut-offs matter. Guidelines change; AI knowledge may be outdated.
  • Verify every clinical fact against current authoritative sources.
