Where to be careful

Hiring decisions carry legal and ethical weight. AI can assist, never decide.

  • Never let AI reject candidates automatically â€” bias in training data becomes bias in hiring
  • Do not upload candidate personal data to third-party tools without consent
  • Use AI to structure evaluation, not to score people
  • Keep a human accountable for every hiring decision
  • Be aware AI can amplify existing bias in your historical hiring data
