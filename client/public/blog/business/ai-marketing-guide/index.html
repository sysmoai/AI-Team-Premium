Strategy before content

Most businesses jump straight to generating posts. Start one level up.

  • "Build a detailed customer persona for [product] in Bangladesh: demographics, daily routine, top three frustrations, and the exact words they use."
  • "What does this customer currently do instead of buying from us?"
  • "List 10 objections ranked by how often they stop a purchase."
  • "Analyse these competitor pages and find the positioning nobody is occupying: [content]"
