Freelancing prompts (Upwork & Fiverr)

  • "Analyse this job post and list the top 3 things the client actually cares about, then write a 150-word proposal that addresses each: [paste job post]"
  • "Rewrite my Fiverr gig description to rank for [keyword] while staying natural and persuasive: [paste description]"
  • "Write 5 Fiverr gig titles under 80 characters for a [service] targeting international buyers."
  • "Draft a polite follow-up message to a client who has not replied in 4 days. Keep it under 60 words."
  • "Turn these rough notes into a professional project update email for my client: [notes]"
  • "Write a price-increase message to an existing client. Justify it with results, keep the tone warm."
  • "Create a 5-question discovery questionnaire I can send new clients before starting a project."

Business and marketing prompts

  • "Write 10 Facebook post ideas for a [business type] in Dhaka. Mix Bengali and English naturally."
  • "Write a Facebook ad in Bengali for [product]. Include a hook, one benefit, social proof and a CTA. Under 100 words."
  • "Create a 30-day content calendar for a [niche] page targeting Bangladeshi customers."
  • "Write three subject lines and one 150-word email announcing our Eid offer."
  • "Act as a business analyst. Given these monthly sales numbers, identify the three biggest problems: [data]"
  • "Write a professional reply to this negative customer review. Apologise without admitting fault: [review]"
  • "Draft a one-page business plan for a [business idea] in Bangladesh with startup cost estimates in BDT."

Study and academic prompts

  • "Explain [concept] in simple Bengali as if I am a class 9 student, with one local example."

  • "Give me 5 memory techniques to remember [list of facts]."
