ChatGPT is a powerful content writing tool. Here's how to use it effectively for blog posts, articles, social media, and more.

Content Writing Workflow with ChatGPT

Step 1: Topic Research

Ask ChatGPT: "What are the trending topics in [your niche] for Bangladeshi audiences?" or "Give me 10 blog post ideas about [topic]."

Step 2: Outline

Ask ChatGPT: "Create a detailed outline for a blog post titled [title]. Include H2 and H3 headings."

Step 3: Write First Draft

Step 4: Edit & Polish

Ask ChatGPT: "Improve this article. Make it more engaging. Add a strong introduction and conclusion."
