Where to find it

  1. Open ChatGPT and click your profile icon (bottom-left).
  2. Choose Customise ChatGPT (or *Custom Instructions*).
  3. You will see two boxes: what ChatGPT should know about you, and how it should respond.
  4. Fill both, save, and start a new chat to see the effect.
