Running a realistic mock interview

The quality of the mock depends entirely on how much context you give:

  • "Act as the hiring manager at a Bangladeshi software company interviewing me for a junior developer role. Ask one question at a time, wait for my answer, then critique it before moving on. Do not go easy on me."
  • "Now ask me five harder follow-up questions based on the weaknesses in my answers."
  • "Ask me the three questions most likely to expose gaps in my CV: [paste CV]"
