Where the ethical line sits

Almost every Bangladeshi university now has an AI policy. The universal principle is simple: AI may assist your thinking; it may not replace it.

Generally acceptableGenerally not acceptable
Brainstorming research questionsSubmitting AI-written text as your own
Explaining a difficult conceptFabricating data or results
Improving grammar and clarityGenerating fake citations
Summarising papers you have readHaving AI write your conclusions
Formatting referencesBypassing the actual learning
