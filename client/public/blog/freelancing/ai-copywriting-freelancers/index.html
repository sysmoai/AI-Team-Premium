Research before writing

Great copy comes from understanding the buyer, not from clever phrasing.

  • "Build a customer persona for [product]: their frustration in their own words, what they have already tried, and why it failed."
  • "List 10 objections, ranked by how often they stop a purchase."
  • "What emotional outcome is this buyer really purchasing?"
  • "Analyse these customer reviews and extract the exact language people use: [reviews]"

Generating and choosing variants

  • "Write this headline 15 ways across different angles."
  • "Write the same sales page opening as: a story, a bold claim, a question, and a statistic."
  • "Rewrite this to address the objection [objection] without mentioning it directly."
  • "Make this 30% shorter with the same persuasive force."
