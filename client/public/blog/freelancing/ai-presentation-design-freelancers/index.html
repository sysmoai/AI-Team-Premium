Narrative before slides

Most bad decks fail on story, not design.

  • "Structure a 12-slide pitch deck for [business]. One message per slide, building to the ask."
  • "What is the single most important thing this audience needs to believe by slide 5?"
  • "Rewrite this deck outline so each slide sets up the next: [outline]"
  • "Cut this 25-slide deck to 12 without losing the argument."

Slide content and speaker notes

  • "For slide 4 on [topic], write a headline that states the conclusion, plus three supporting points."
  • "Write speaker notes for this slide: what to say, timing, and the transition to the next."
  • "Turn this dense paragraph into a slide with a headline and three bullets: [text]"
  • "Generate 10 likely audience questions with suggested answers."
