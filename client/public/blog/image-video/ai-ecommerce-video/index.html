Workflow for a product video

  1. Start with a clean product photo on a plain background
  2. Upload to an AI video tool and describe the motion you want
  3. Generate multiple short clips (5-10 seconds each)
  4. Assemble in CapCut or a simple editor
  5. Add text overlays highlighting key features and price
  6. Export in 9:16 for Reels and Shorts, or 16:9 for Daraz

Tips for Bangladeshi sellers
