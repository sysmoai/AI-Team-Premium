Tree of thought

Instead of one line of reasoning, force the model to explore several and compare them.

  • "Give me three fundamentally different approaches to [problem]. For each: how it works, main risk, and cost. Then recommend one and justify it."
  • Best for strategy, architecture and business decisions where the obvious answer may not be best.

Self-consistency

Solve the same problem multiple times independently and check whether the answers agree.

  • "Solve this three separate ways. If the answers differ, identify where the reasoning diverged."
  • Best for maths, financial calculations and logic where a silent error is costly.
