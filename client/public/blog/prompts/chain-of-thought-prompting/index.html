The technique

Add one instruction: "Think through this step by step before giving your final answer." That is the entire method.

Without CoTWith CoT
"What is 15% of 2,340 taka?""Calculate 15% of 2,340 taka. Show each step, then state the final answer."
"Should I hire this candidate?""Evaluate this candidate step by step: strengths, gaps, risk factors, then a recommendation."
"Is this contract fair?""Analyse this contract clause by clause, flag anything unusual, then summarise."
