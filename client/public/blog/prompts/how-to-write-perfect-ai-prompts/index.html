Five mistakes that ruin output

  1. Being vague. "Write something good" gives you something generic.
  2. Asking for too much at once. Split multi-part jobs into separate prompts.
  3. Not specifying format. Without it you get a wall of prose.
  4. Never iterating. The second prompt is usually where the quality appears.
  5. Not giving examples. One sample of what you want beats three paragraphs describing it.
