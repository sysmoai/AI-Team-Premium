Reporting and summarising

  • "Turn these raw numbers into a one-page monthly report for a non-technical owner. Lead with the three things that matter: [data]"
  • "Summarise this 20-page report into 10 bullet points, then into one sentence."
  • "Write the executive summary last â€” after reading this full document: [document]"
  • "Convert these meeting notes into decisions, owners and deadlines: [notes]"

Financial and operational analysis

  • "Analyse this P&L. Where is money leaking? Rank by size of opportunity: [data]"
  • "Model three pricing scenarios for [product] and show the break-even point for each."
  • "Given these costs, what is my true unit economics including overhead?"
  • "What are the five biggest operational risks in this process and how do I mitigate each?"

Strategy and decisions

  • "I am deciding between [A] and [B]. Build the case for each, then argue against your own preferred option."
  • "You are a sceptical investor. Find every hole in this business plan: [plan]"
  • "What would have to be true for this strategy to work? Which of those assumptions is weakest?"
  • "Give me a pre-mortem: it is one year later and this failed. What went wrong?"

Hiring and people

  • "Write a job description for [role] at a Bangladeshi [industry] company. Realistic salary range in BDT."
  • "Create 10 interview questions that reveal actual ability rather than rehearsed answers."
  • "Design a 30-60-90 day onboarding plan for a new [role]."
  • "Write constructive performance feedback for someone who is [situation]."
