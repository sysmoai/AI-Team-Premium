What is a Prompt?

A prompt is the instruction you give to an AI model. A good prompt is specific, provides context, and clearly states what you want. Think of it like giving instructions to a brilliant but very literal assistant.

Simple Prompt Formula

Use this formula: "Act as a [ROLE]. Your task is to [TASK]. Context: [CONTEXT]. Output in [FORMAT]. Constraints: [CONSTRAINTS]."
