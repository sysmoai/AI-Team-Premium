Killing the generic AI voice

The tell-tale markers are predictable. Ban them explicitly.

  • "Do not use: delve, tapestry, testament, landscape, realm, navigate, unlock, elevate, in today's fast-paced world."
  • "Vary sentence length. Some short. Some longer with a clause that adds texture."
  • "No summary paragraph at the end that repeats what you just said."
  • "Write like a knowledgeable person talking, not like a corporate blog."
  • "Use concrete specifics instead of general claims."
