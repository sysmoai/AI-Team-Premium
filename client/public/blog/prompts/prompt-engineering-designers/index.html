The six-part image prompt

ComponentPurposeExample
SubjectWhat is in frame"A young Bangladeshi woman in a red sari"
Action/poseWhat is happening"walking through a tea garden, looking back"
EnvironmentWhere"misty Sylhet hills at sunrise"
LightingMood driver"soft golden hour backlight"
StyleVisual treatment"photorealistic, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field"
CompositionFraming"medium shot, subject right of centre, negative space left"

Style keywords that reliably work

  • Photography: photorealistic, 35mm/85mm lens, shallow depth of field, studio lighting, golden hour, high key, low key
  • Illustration: flat vector, line art, watercolour, gouache, isometric, cel-shaded
  • 3D: octane render, clay render, product visualisation, soft studio HDRI
  • Mood: cinematic, moody, vibrant, minimal, editorial, dramatic

Leaving space for text

The most common practical mistake in marketing visuals is generating a perfect image with nowhere to put the headline.

  • Always specify: "generous negative space on the left for text overlay"
  • For thumbnails: "subject on the right third, clean background on the left"
  • For banners: "wide composition, low visual noise in the centre"
