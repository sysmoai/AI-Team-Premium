Tailoring to the job

  • "Here is a job description and my CV. List the keywords I am missing and exactly where to add them naturally: [JD] [CV]"
  • "Rewrite my professional summary specifically for this role: [JD]"
  • "Which of my experiences are most relevant to this job? Rank them and tell me what to cut."
  • "Is my CV ATS-friendly? List every formatting problem."

Cover letters that get read

  • "Write a cover letter for [role] at [company]. Open with why *this* company, not a generic statement. Under 250 words."
  • "Rewrite this cover letter to lead with my strongest achievement instead of my background: [letter]"
  • "Write a version for a company that values [culture trait based on their website]."
