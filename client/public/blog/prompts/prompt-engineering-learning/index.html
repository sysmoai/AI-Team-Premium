The Feynman technique with AI

Explain a concept simply; wherever you stumble is where you do not understand it.

  1. "I will explain [concept] in my own words. Identify every gap or error in my understanding: [your explanation]"
  2. "Now explain the parts I got wrong, simply."
  3. "Ask me three questions that would expose whether I really understand this."
  4. "I will explain again â€” grade my second attempt."

Active recall

  • "Quiz me on [topic]. One question at a time. Wait for my answer before revealing the correct one."
  • "Generate 20 questions ranging from basic recall to application. Do not show answers yet."
  • "I got these wrong: [list]. Diagnose the underlying misunderstanding and drill me on it."

Spaced repetition

  • "Create a spaced repetition schedule for these 30 facts over 30 days: [facts]"

Learning a new skill

  • "I want to learn [skill] in 90 days, 1 hour daily. Build a curriculum with weekly milestones and projects."
  • "What are the 20% of concepts in [field] that deliver 80% of the practical value?"
  • "What do beginners in [skill] typically get wrong, and how do I avoid it?"
  • "Give me a project that would prove I have actually learned this."
