Start with the customer, not the product

Never ask for copy before the model understands who is reading it.

  • "Build a detailed customer persona for [product] in Bangladesh: age, income, daily routine, top three frustrations, and the exact words they would use to describe the problem."
  • "List 10 objections a Bangladeshi buyer would have about [product], ranked by how often they occur."
  • "What does this customer currently do instead of buying my product?"

Ad copy with structure

  • "Write a Facebook ad for [product] targeting [persona]. Structure: pattern-interrupt hook, one core benefit, proof, price in BDT, CTA. Under 90 words. Natural Banglish."
  • "Give me the same ad in five angles: problem/solution, curiosity, social proof, urgency, identity."
  • "Write only the first line, ten different ways."
  • "Rewrite this ad to address the objection: [objection]"

Landing pages and email

  • "Write landing page copy for [offer]: headline, subhead, three benefit blocks, objection handling, FAQ, CTA."
  • "Write a five-email welcome sequence for [business]. Email 1 delivers value, 5 sells. Subject lines under 45 characters."
  • "Write a cart-abandonment email that does not sound desperate."
  • "Write three subject line variants: curiosity, benefit, urgency."

Analysis prompts

  • "Here is our ad performance data. Which creative is winning and what does that suggest about our audience? [data]"
  • "Analyse this competitor's messaging and identify the angle they are not using: [copy]"
  • "Critique this ad as a sceptical customer would."
