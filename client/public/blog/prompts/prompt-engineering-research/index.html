Synthesising what you have read

Read the papers yourself. Then use AI to see across them.

  • "Here are summaries of eight papers on [topic]. Identify common findings, contradictions and unanswered questions: [summaries]"
  • "Group these studies by methodology and tell me which approach dominates the field."
  • "What does the evidence actually support, and where are researchers over-claiming?"
  • "Build a chronological narrative of how thinking on [topic] has evolved."

Finding the gap

  • "Based on this literature, what has not been studied? Rank gaps by research value."
  • "What would a study on [topic] in the Bangladeshi context add that existing Western studies miss?"
  • "Formulate three research questions that are specific, answerable and original."

Methodology

  • "For this research question, compare quantitative and qualitative approaches. Which is more appropriate and why? [question]"
  • "Design a survey instrument for [topic]. Flag any leading questions."
  • "What sample size do I need for [study design]? Show the reasoning."
  • "What are the main threats to validity in this design and how do I mitigate them?"
