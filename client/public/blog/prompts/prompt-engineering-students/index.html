Understanding difficult concepts

  • "Explain [concept] at three levels: to a 10-year-old, to a first-year student, and to an expert."
  • "Explain [concept] in simple Bengali with one example from daily life in Bangladesh."
  • "I understand [X] but not [Y]. Bridge the gap between them."
  • "Draw an analogy between [difficult concept] and something familiar to a Bangladeshi student."
  • "What is the single most common misunderstanding about [topic]?"

Active recall and practice testing

Re-reading notes feels productive and barely works. Testing yourself works. AI makes unlimited testing free.

  • "Generate 15 exam-style questions on [topic] with answers hidden below. I will attempt them first."
  • "Quiz me on [topic] one question at a time. Wait for my answer before revealing the correct one."
  • "I got this wrong: [answer]. Explain my error and give me three similar questions to practise."
  • "Create a mock exam paper for [subject] matching the [board/university] format."

Essay and assignment feedback

  • "Grade this essay out of 100 against these criteria: [criteria]. Give specific improvements, not general praise: [essay]"
  • "Identify every unsupported claim in this essay and tell me what evidence it needs."
  • "Is my argument logically consistent? Point out any contradictions."
  • "Rewrite my weakest paragraph and explain what you changed and why."

Study planning

  • "Build a 30-day study plan for [exam]. I have 3 hours daily. Include revision days and a mock test."
  • "I have 5 subjects and 2 weeks. Help me prioritise based on my current weakness: [details]"
  • "Break this syllabus into daily tasks I can actually finish: [syllabus]"
