Teachers and educators

  • "Create a lesson plan for [topic] for class [X], 40 minutes, including one activity and an assessment."
  • "Generate 20 exam questions on [topic] across difficulty levels with an answer key."
  • "Explain [concept] three ways for students with different learning styles."
  • "Write constructive feedback for a student who [situation]."
  • "Turn this syllabus into a term-by-term teaching schedule: [syllabus]"

Doctors and healthcare workers

  • "Explain [condition] to a patient in simple Bengali, avoiding medical jargon."
  • "Write patient discharge instructions for [procedure] in Bengali."
  • "Summarise the current guidance on [topic] and note where sources disagree."
  • "Draft a referral letter with these details: [details]"
