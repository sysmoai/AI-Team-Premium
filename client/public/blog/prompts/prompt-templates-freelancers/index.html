Winning the job

  • Proposal: "Analyse this job post and identify the client's top 3 concerns. Then write a 150-word proposal addressing each. Reference my experience in [skill]. Warm and confident, not salesy: [job post]"
  • Follow-up: "Write a short follow-up to a client who viewed my proposal but did not reply. Add one new piece of value. Under 60 words."
  • Portfolio pitch: "Write a one-paragraph case study of this project focused on the client's result, not my process: [project]"
  • Gig description: "Write a Fiverr gig description for [service]. Cover what they get, why me, process, delivery time and an FAQ."

Running the project

  • Scope doc: "Turn this client conversation into a clear scope: deliverables, exclusions, timeline, revisions, payment terms: [conversation]"
  • Progress update: "Write a brief client update. 70% complete, minor delay on [item], delivering Friday. Reassuring, not apologetic."
  • Clarifying questions: "What five questions should I ask before starting this project? [brief]"
  • Scope creep: "Write a polite message explaining that [request] is outside our agreed scope, with a quote to add it."
