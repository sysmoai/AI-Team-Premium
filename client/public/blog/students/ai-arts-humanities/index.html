Building arguments, not outsourcing them

  • "Here is my thesis statement. What are the three strongest objections to it? [thesis]"
  • "Argue against my position as persuasively as you can."
  • "What evidence would I need to make this argument convincing?"
  • "Is my argument actually original, or am I restating a standard position?"
  • "What theoretical framework would strengthen this analysis?"

Literary and textual analysis

  • "What critical approaches have been applied to [work], and what does each reveal?"
  • "Compare how [author A] and [author B] treat [theme]."
  • "What is the historical context I need to understand this text properly?"
