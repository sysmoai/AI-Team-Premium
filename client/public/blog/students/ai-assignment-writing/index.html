The right sequence

Do your thinking first. Use AI second. That single ordering keeps you safe and makes you better.

  1. Read the question properly â€” "Break this assignment question into what it is actually asking."
  2. Form your own answer before opening AI. Even a rough one.
  3. Stress-test it â€” "Here is my argument. What is wrong with it?"
  4. Research what you need â€” read the sources yourself.
  5. Write your draft in your own words.
  6. Get feedback â€” "Grade this and list specific weaknesses."
  7. Revise based on the feedback, in your own words again.
