Case study analysis

Read the case yourself first, form a view, then use AI to pressure-test it.

  • "Here is my analysis of this case. What have I missed? Be critical: [analysis]"
  • "Apply Porter's Five Forces to this case and tell me which force is actually binding."
  • "What would the opposing recommendation be, and how strong is it?"
  • "What data would I need to be confident in this recommendation?"
  • "What is the second-order consequence of this strategy that most students would miss?"

Financial analysis

  • "Calculate the key ratios from these financials and interpret what they say: [data]"
  • "Build a simple DCF model for this scenario and state every assumption."
  • "Explain why this company's margins declined based on these statements."
  • "What red flags appear in these financials?"
  • "Explain [financial concept] with a Bangladeshi company as the example."
