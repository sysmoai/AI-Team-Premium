Testing yourself is roughly twice as effective as re-reading for the same time invested.

  • "Quiz me on [topic]. One question at a time. Wait for my answer before revealing the correct one."

  • "Ask me the three questions most likely to expose gaps in my understanding of [topic]."

Mock exams

  • "Create a full mock paper for [exam] matching the [board/university] format, mark distribution and time limit."
