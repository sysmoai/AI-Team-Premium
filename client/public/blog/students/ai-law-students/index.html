The citation problem is worse in law

AI will produce case names, citations and holdings that sound entirely plausible and do not exist. This has ended careers.

  • Never cite a case you have not read in an authoritative database
  • AI knowledge of Bangladeshi law specifically is thin â€” training data is dominated by US and UK sources
  • Statutes change; AI training cut-offs mean it may cite repealed provisions
  • Use AI for reasoning structure, not for finding authority
