Phase 1 â€” Finding sources safely

Use Perplexity Pro, not ChatGPT. Perplexity retrieves real pages and links to them. ChatGPT generates from memory and can invent references.

  • "Find peer-reviewed research on [topic] from the last five years. Include citations."
  • "What are the most-cited papers on [topic]?"
  • "Is there research specifically on [topic] in a Bangladeshi or South Asian context?"
  • Then open every single link before using it.

Phase 2 â€” Understanding what you read

Read the paper. Then use AI to check your understanding.

  • "How does this paper's finding relate to [other paper's finding]?"

Phase 3 â€” Synthesis

This is where AI adds the most value. Feed it your own summaries, not raw papers you have not read.

  • "Here are my summaries of 10 papers. Identify common findings, contradictions and unanswered questions: [summaries]"
  • "Group these studies by methodology and tell me which approach dominates."
  • "Build a chronological narrative of how thinking on this topic has evolved."
  • "What gap in this literature would a Bangladeshi study fill?"

Data analysis

  • "Clean this dataset and flag anomalies: [data]"
  • "Which statistical test is appropriate here and why?"
  • "Run the analysis, show the working, and interpret the result plainly."
  • "Produce a chart suitable for an academic paper with labelled axes."
  • "What are the limitations of this analysis I should state?"
