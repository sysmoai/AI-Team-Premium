Building speed, not just accuracy

Admission tests are won on speed. Most candidates know the material and run out of time.

  • "Give me 20 questions on [topic] and tell me the time limit for each."
  • "Show me the fastest method for this problem type."
  • "What shortcuts do top scorers use for [topic]?"
  • "Time me: give one question, wait for my answer, then the next."
  • "Which question types should I skip and come back to?"
ðŸŽ¯ Practise skipping. A key admission-test skill nobody teaches: recognising within ten seconds that a question will take too long. Ask AI to drill you on identifying which questions to defer.
