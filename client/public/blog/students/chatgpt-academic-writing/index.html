Feedback prompts that actually improve your work

Vague requests get vague praise. These force specificity.

  • "Grade this essay out of 100 against these criteria: [criteria]. Justify every deduction: [essay]"
  • "Identify every claim I made without supporting evidence."
  • "Where is my argument logically weakest? Be harsh."
  • "What would a critical examiner attack first?"
  • "Rewrite my weakest paragraph and explain exactly what you changed and why."

Citation safety

The most common way students get caught is fabricated references. Language models generate citations that look perfectly formatted and do not exist.
