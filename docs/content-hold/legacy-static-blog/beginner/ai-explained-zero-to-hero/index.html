Chapter 1: The Absolute Basics (একদম বেসিক)

At its core, Artificial Intelligence is about making machines smart. But what does "smart" mean for a computer? A computer is smart when it can:

  • See — recognize objects in images (computer vision)
  • Hear — understand spoken words (speech recognition)
  • Read — understand text and language (NLP)
  • Think — make decisions and solve problems (reasoning)
  • Create — generate new content (generative AI)

Chapter 2: The Building Blocks

Data — The Fuel of AI

AI runs on data. The more quality data it has, the better it performs. Think of AI as an engine and data as the fuel. Without data, AI is just empty code. AI চালানোর জন্য ডেটা প্রয়োজন। ডেটা হলো AI-এর জ্বালানি।

Algorithms — The Engine

Algorithms are step-by-step instructions that tell the AI how to learn from data. Different algorithms are good at different tasks — some excel at recognizing patterns in images, others at understanding language.

Compute Power — The Accelerator

AI needs powerful computers to process all that data. Modern AI uses GPUs (Graphics Processing Units) — the same chips that power video games — to run millions of calculations simultaneously.

Chapter 3: Machine Learning — How AI Learns

Machine Learning (ML) is the most important subset of AI. Instead of being explicitly programmed for every task, ML systems learn from examples. There are three main types:

TypeHow It WorksBangladesh Example
Supervised LearningAI learns from labeled examplesTraining AI to recognize Bengali handwritten digits
Unsupervised LearningAI finds patterns in unlabeled dataGrouping customers by shopping behavior on Daraz
Reinforcement LearningAI learns through trial and errorAI learning to play Ludo or Chess

Chapter 4: Deep Learning — The Brain of Modern AI

Deep Learning is a more advanced form of machine learning that uses neural networks — computing systems inspired by the human brain. These networks have multiple "layers" that process information at increasing levels of abstraction.

ডিপ লার্নিং হল মেশিন লার্নিং-এর একটি উন্নত রূপ যা নিউরাল নেটওয়ার্ক ব্যবহার করে — মানব মস্তিষ্ক দ্বারা অনুপ্রাণিত কম্পিউটিং সিস্টেম।

Deep Learning is what powers ChatGPT, Midjourney, Google Translate, and virtually every impressive AI application you've used.

Chapter 5: Generative AI — The Creative Revolution

Generative AI is the technology that creates — text, images, music, video, and code. Unlike traditional AI that only analyzes or classifies, generative AI produces original content. This is the technology behind:

  • ChatGPT — generates text, code, analysis
  • Midjourney / DALL-E — generates images from text
  • ElevenLabs — generates human-like speech
  • Runway / Pika — generates video from text
