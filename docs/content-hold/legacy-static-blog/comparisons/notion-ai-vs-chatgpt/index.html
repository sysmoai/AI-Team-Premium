Notion AI's real strength

It has context on *your* content. It can summarise a meeting note you wrote last month, auto-fill a database property, or turn a page of bullets into a brief — without you pasting anything.

  • Summarise any page or meeting note in place
  • Auto-generate database fields (status, priority, summary) across many rows
  • Turn rough notes into structured documents
  • Translate whole pages inline

ChatGPT's real strength

  • Reasoning about problems Notion has no data on
  • Analysing spreadsheets and generating charts
  • Creating images, code and long-form content
  • Far better Bengali handling
  • Voice conversations
💡 If you must choose one: ChatGPT. Notion AI is excellent *if you already live in Notion*. ChatGPT is more capable at more things for less money. Buy ChatGPT first; add Notion AI only if Notion is your primary workspace.
