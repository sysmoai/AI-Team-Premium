What AI video is actually good for today

  • B-roll and background footage — no shoot required

  • Social media clips — Reels, Shorts, TikTok

What it is still bad at

  • Consistent human faces across a long sequence
  • Readable text inside video frames
  • Precise, repeatable physics and hand movement
  • Clips longer than about ten seconds without visible drift
💡 The realistic 2026 workflow. Generate short clips, then assemble them in a normal editor. AI supplies the shots; you still supply the edit.
