The professional translation workflow

  1. Context first — "This is a [document type] for [audience]. Translate to natural Bengali, ভাব অনুবাদ not আক্ষরিক."
  2. Generate the first pass with Claude Pro (best Bengali) or ChatGPT Plus.
  3. Flag uncertainty — "Mark any phrase where you were unsure of the best rendering."
  4. Human edit — this is where your value is. Fix register, idiom, cultural fit.
  5. Back-translate check — "Translate this back to English so I can verify meaning survived."
  6. Final read — does it sound like a Bengali speaker wrote it?

What AI gets wrong in Bengali

Knowing these keeps you employable.

  • Register drift — defaults to formal সাধু when চলিত is needed
  • Idioms — translates literally, losing the meaning entirely
  • Cultural context — misses references a Bangladeshi reader assumes
  • Regional dialect — Sylheti and Chittagonian are weakly handled
  • Formal document conventions — legal and government Bengali has strict forms
  • Tone for the specific audience — cannot judge who is reading
✅ This is your job security. AI produces grammatically correct Bengali. It cannot judge whether that Bengali is *right for this reader*. That judgement is what clients pay a human translator for.

Repricing translation work

  • Stop pricing per word — AI made raw word count meaningless
  • Offer tiers: machine-assisted (fast, cheap) vs full human-edited (premium)
  • Add services — localisation, cultural adaptation, transcreation
  • Take volume — 6× throughput means 6× the clients at the same hours
  • Specialise — legal, medical and technical Bengali command far higher rates
