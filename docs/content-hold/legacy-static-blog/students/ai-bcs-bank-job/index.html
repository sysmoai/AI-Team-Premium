MCQ practice at unlimited volume

  • "Generate 25 BCS-style MCQs on [topic] with four options each. Do not show answers yet."
  • "Now give the answers with a one-line explanation for each."
  • "I got questions 3, 7 and 12 wrong. Explain those concepts and give me 5 more on each."
  • "Create a full BCS preliminary mock in the standard format and mark distribution."

⚠️ Verify current affairs. AI training data has a cut-off. For current affairs, recent appointments, and latest statistics, always verify against newspapers and official sources. Do not rely on AI for anything time-sensitive.

Maths shortcuts under time pressure

  • "Show me the fastest method for this problem type — I have 40 seconds: [problem]"
  • "Teach me the shortcut techniques for [topic] used in competitive exams."
